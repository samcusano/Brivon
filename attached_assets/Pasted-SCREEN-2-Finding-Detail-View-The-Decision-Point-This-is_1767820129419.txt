Key Design Decisions:

Information hierarchy:

Summary first (what's wrong)
AI analysis second (what the system thinks)
Evidence third (prove it to me)
Context fourth (business implications)
Decision last (now act)


Confidence explanation:

Shows percentage upfront
"Why 92%?" is expandable to show: "Based on 47 similar cases, policy match confidence 98%, evidence quality 94%, vendor context 85%"


Evidence is directly accessible:

Click to view PDFs, emails, policies inline
No need to leave this screen to verify
Timestamps on everything (when was this checked?)


AI reasoning is explicit:

Bullet points, not paragraphs
Cites specific policies and thresholds
Shows the logic chain


Decision options are structured but flexible:

Radio buttons force a choice
Accept is easy (fewest clicks)
Override requires work (dropdown + justification) - intentional friction for audit
"Need more info" lets agent do more work before deciding
Escalate is always available


Audit trail built-in:

Every override requires justification
Timestamps everywhere
Who/what/when/why captured automatically
