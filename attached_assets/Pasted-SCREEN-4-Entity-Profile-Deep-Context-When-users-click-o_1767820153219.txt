Key Design Decisions:

Risk score with trend: Shows trajectory, not just current state
Tabs for different views: Findings vs. Assessments vs. Documents (user chooses their focus)
Decision history front and center: Full audit trail of AI recommendations + human decisions
Overrides are clearly marked: Users can see when they disagreed with AI and why
