Key Design Decisions:

Summary cards at top: Gives the "30-second answer" - How many urgent items? What are agents doing?
Queue uses visual priority: Red/Yellow/Green + icons for scanability
Each item shows:

Entity ID + Name (for traceability)
Plain English summary (not jargon)
AI recommendation with confidence (transparency)
Time pressure (SLA/deadline)
Ownership (accountability)


"Review Finding" is the primary CTA: Not "Approve" or "Reject" - we want them to investigate first
